A Large-Scale Continuously Sampled UAV LiDAR–Camera Multimodal Dataset and Construction Benchmark

Abstract

Constructing high-quality large-scale UAV LiDAR–camera multimodal datasets is fundamental to three-dimensional scene understanding, low-altitude intelligence, and large-scale map reconstruction.

We present UAVTraffic3D, a large-scale continuously sampled UAV LiDAR–camera multimodal dataset covering six representative urban traffic scenarios. The dataset contains synchronized multimodal observations, calibration parameters, static scene representations, and dynamic trajectories.

We further introduce a unified dataset construction pipeline integrating temporal synchronization, frame matching, progressive calibration, quality control, static mapping, and dynamic trajectory generation.

Dataset Construction Pipeline

The pipeline consists of temporal synchronization, sampling-aware frame matching, progressive LiDAR–camera calibration, quality control, dynamic-object removal, static mapping, and multi-frame trajectory generation.

Progressive LiDAR–Camera Calibration

Our calibration framework follows a progressive coarse-to-fine strategy and jointly considers field-of-view coverage, reprojection consistency, and cross-modal edge alignment. Static and quasi-stationary UAV observations are used to improve optimization robustness under long-range sensing, sparse LiDAR observations, platform vibration, and complex 6-DoF motion.

Benchmark Tasks

Calibration

Evaluation of LiDAR–camera geometric alignment, reprojection quality, edge consistency, and field-of-view coverage.

Static Mapping

Evaluation of static scene reconstruction after synchronization, calibration, and dynamic-object removal.

Dynamic Trajectories

Evaluation of multi-frame dynamic-object reconstruction and temporally consistent three-dimensional trajectories.

Results

Quantitative and qualitative results demonstrate the effectiveness and robustness of the proposed construction pipeline across diverse UAV traffic scenes.
